Content Engine Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Content Engine is a private desktop tool. One person runs it on their own computer to create short videos and send them to their own TikTok account. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What data the app uses

  • TikTok login data: after you connect your TikTok account, the app receives an access token and the scopes you approved (basic profile information, video upload, and, if granted, your own video list and account statistics).
  • Your own videos: video files you create with the app and choose to upload.
  • Statistics: view, like, comment and share counts of your own videos, used to show you how they perform.

Where the data is stored

Everything stays on your own computer, in a local database. Access tokens are stored encrypted. The app has no server, does not collect analytics about you, and does not send your data to the developer or anyone else.

Who the data is shared with

Data is only sent to TikTok, when you start an upload or when the app fetches statistics of your own videos through TikTok's official API. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

Your control

  • Every upload needs your explicit approval in the app.
  • You can disconnect TikTok in the app at any time, which deletes the stored tokens.
  • You can also revoke access in your TikTok settings under Security and permissions.
  • Deleting the app's data folder removes all stored data.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated.
